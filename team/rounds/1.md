**Aim (conductor, 2026-11-18).** Set up the team and build the whole site once: the billboard screen on live Algarve data with London (simulated) and Porto, Braga and Lisbon (live IPMA) as the screen's location; the scripted planning assistant; the idea, credited to Dinis Cruz; five designs; Use the data; Use with AI; behind the scenes; link previews. Dinis's words are in `team/BRIEF.md`, with the times given, including the 18:00 UTC addition: "This simulation of a billboard, which could be in London or other parts of Portugal too (since the weather is usually much better there) will be on one of our ACC.live sites. And will be 100% client side driven."

## The contract every role builds to (conductor, round 1)

Pages and layout
- Every page in `src/` is a full HTML file: `<html lang="en-GB">`, a `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and the markers `<!--HEAD-->` (inside head), `<!--HEADER-->` (first thing in body) and `<!--FOOTER-->` (last thing in body). `scripts/layout.mjs` fills them from `scripts/lib.mjs`: canonical, link-preview tags, `/assets/site.css`, the simulation band, the navigation, the footer with the data attribution and the version.
- A page picks its share card with `<meta name="share-image" content="/assets/share/<name>.png">` (1200x630, under 300 KB, made by `scripts/cards.mjs`, committed). Default `/assets/share/home.png`.
- A page that reads data lists the source ids on `<body data-sources="ipma-observations ipma-city-forecasts ...">` so its Markdown twin can list them.
- Navigation: `/` The screen, `/idea/` The idea, `/plan/` Planning assistant (simulation), `/d/` Designs, `/use-the-data/` Use the data, `/use-with-ai/` Use with AI, `/behind-the-scenes/` Behind the scenes.
- The band on every page, word for word: "Simulation. This billboard does not exist: no screen, campaign or booking is real. The Algarve weather is real, read live from IPMA via data.promptugal.news; London's weather is set by you; flights, prices and times are invented examples. Idea: Dinis Cruz."

Data (read live in the browser; never copied into the repository beyond small test fixtures)
- Base `https://data.promptugal.news/api/v1`. Algarve side: `by-place/now/<place>.json` (faro, albufeira, tavira, quarteira (for Vilamoura), portimao, lagos), each value with its own source and time read. Cards: `<source>/card.json` (publisher, cadence, terms).
- Portuguese "here" side: `ipma-city-forecasts/latest.json` (Porto 1131200, Braga 1030300, Lisboa 1110600) and `ipma-observations/latest.json` (nearest reporting station to the city; Braga's own stations may be silent: say which station is used and how far).
- Beside every figure: source, publisher, time read. Stale (older than twice the card's cadence) or missing: say so on the page, never guess a value. IPMA: always named; no profit-making use.
- London: no source exists on 2026-11-18. A control sets weather, time and station; every London figure is labelled "simulated".

Labels
- Live values: "live". Set by the visitor: "simulated". Flights, prices, times: "invented example". The drawn scene: "drawing, not a camera: live.promptugal.news offers no cameras yet (checked 2026-11-18)". The planning assistant: "simulation: scripted answers, no live model".

Writing
- Plain British English, no em dashes, no hype words, they/them. Name no person but Dinis Cruz. Name no tourism body, operator, airline or advertiser as involved. White paper figures only with a primary source or marked "source unconfirmed".
